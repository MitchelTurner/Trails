---
title: Why we made a map before a mission statement
date: 2026-08-01
excerpt: You cannot argue about a paragraph. You can point at a gap.
---

A mission statement is a thing you write so a group feels organized. It does not tell anybody
where the trail stops.

So we drew it instead. Every piece of tread on Revilla we could get linework for, every stretch
we want to connect, and — this is the part that matters in a district office — who owns the
ground under each mile. A line that leaves Forest Service land, clips a State selection, and
finishes on Borough dirt is not a detail. It is the whole reason the island was never stitched
together.

Three sets of people need to look at the same thing. Somebody who already walks *Ward Creek*
wants to know where it goes next. A district ranger wants to know whose ground you are
proposing to cross and whether you understand what that means. A funder wants to see a project
they can put money against, not a feeling.

One map does all three. A paragraph does none of them.

The pink is what does not exist yet. That is deliberate, and every pink line on this site says
so on its own page.
