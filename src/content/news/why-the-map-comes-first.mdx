---
title: Why the map comes first
date: 2026-08-01
excerpt: A mission statement cannot carry "connect the island." A status-coded map can.
---

The association is not a hiking club. The work is connective public infrastructure: existing
trails, the gaps between them, and the land managers who sit on both.

A paragraph cannot show a Forest Service district ranger, a Borough assembly member, and a
future funder the same artifact. A map can. That is why the homepage is the network, and why
every proposed line on it says, out loud, that it is not surveyed.

If you already walk *Rainbird*, *Ward Lake*, or *Deer Mountain*, you are looking at the built
miles. The pink is what does not exist yet.
