---
title: There is no donate button
date: 2026-08-12
excerpt: No entity, no bank account, nothing deductible. Your name is worth more to us right now anyway.
---

We are not set up to take money, so we are not going to ask for it.

There is no incorporated entity behind this yet. That means no bank account, no liability
separation, and nothing you sent would be tax-deductible no matter how nicely a page was
worded. Alaska also expects an organization to register with the Department of Law before it
solicits contributions at all. Putting a payment button up before any of that is squared away
is how a group gets a reputation it cannot walk back.

What we ask for instead is your name and a ZIP code. That sounds like less. It is not. When
somebody from the group sits down with the Ketchikan–Misty Fjords Ranger District or stands up
at an assembly meeting, the useful thing to have is a list of residents who want the route —
and a count of how many of them live in the borough.

When there is an entity with a bank account, we will say so here, plainly, and we will state
what is and is not deductible. Until then: sign on, and come out on a work party.
