---
title: There is no donate button
date: 2026-08-12
excerpt: Until there is an entity, donated money would land in a personal account. We will not do that.
---

Phase 1 has no payment processor. The group is informal. There is no bank account, no liability
separation, and nothing is tax-deductible. Alaska also requires charitable-solicitation
registration before an organization asks for contributions.

What we have instead is a public supporter count. Sign on. That number is what we put in front
of the Assembly and the Forest Service.

The two ordinary paths, when the board is ready: incorporate as an Alaska nonprofit, or operate
under a fiscal sponsor. Until one of those exists, we will not imply that money sent to us is a
charitable gift.
