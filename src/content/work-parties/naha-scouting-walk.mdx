Walk the six miles up to the Heckman Lake cabin and pick up the shore line from the end of the
mapped tread. We are recording tracks, photographing the wet stretches, and marking where
puncheon will be needed.

Weather-dependent on the boat shuttle. Nothing gets cut on this trip — this is a scouting walk
for the survey that has to come before the environmental study.
