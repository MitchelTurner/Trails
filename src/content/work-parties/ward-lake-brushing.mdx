Brush and tread work on the Nature Trail and the first mile of *Ward Creek*. Alder and salmonberry
have closed in on both sides and there are a couple of soft spots in the boardwalk.

This is maintenance on an existing public facility, not new construction. No saw card needed —
most of the work is loppers and a shovel.
