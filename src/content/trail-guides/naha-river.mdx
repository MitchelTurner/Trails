title: Naha River National Recreation Trail — trail guide
updated: 2026-08-28

The Naha River National Recreation Trail runs **5.8 miles** from the floating dock at
the head of Naha Bay to the cabin site at the outlet of Heckman Lake. Roughly the
lower 2.5 miles is near-flat boardwalk paralleling Roosevelt Lagoon; the grade steepens
moderately for the remaining miles to Heckman. The trailhead is reachable by boat or
floatplane only — about 23 miles from Ketchikan, 12 miles from Knudson Cove.

_(The map length above is measured from the clipped GIS line; the Forest Service posts
the trail at 5.8 miles end to end.)_

## Cabins on the route

- **Jordan Lake Cabin** — about 4 miles in, two miles down-trail from Heckman. Not
  reachable by most floatplanes, so people fly to Heckman and walk in.
- **Heckman Lake Cabin** — trail terminus at 5.8 miles, near the outlet of Heckman Lake.
  Pan-Abode, built 1967, sleeps 6, with a 14-foot skiff and oars.
- **Southeast Heckman Cabin** — a separate, larger cabin (16'x22', sleeps 8) on the
  southeast shore. It is floatplane-access only; the trail does not serve it.

## Before you go

The trail crosses the **private grounds of Orton Ranch** about two miles in. It is a
working residence, not a viewpoint — stay on the trail and be respectful of the people
who live there. Continued public use of this route depends on it.

## The salt chuck

The narrow outlet of Roosevelt Lagoon turns into a roaring whitewater chute as the tide
moves in or out. **Do not attempt to run or wade it.** Use the portage around the
rapids; there is a shelter with a picnic table near the outlet.

## Bear

This is an active salmon river July through September. Make noise, carry what you carry,
and know how to use it.

## Fishing

Steelhead spring and fall — the Naha is especially known for steelhead — salmon July
through September, and resident Dolly Varden and cutthroat year-round in the river and
in Jordan and Heckman lakes. Check the current ADF&G regulations before you go.
